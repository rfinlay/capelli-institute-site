BIPP Education Series

Domestic violence intervention & prevention

About the program

Battering Intervention and Prevention Program

An 18-week program that satisfies the 36-hour TDCJ-CJAD and State of Texas requirement. Four phases, in English and in Spanish.

BIPP is not an anger management program. It addresses socio-cultural issues regarding gender and violence, critical thinking, empathy, accountability, and communication.

the BIPP Education Series description for the Institute site, in the author's words: what the series is, who it is for, and what a participant receives on completion.

How enrollment works

  1. Call. Phone the office to schedule an evaluation and orientation.
  2. Evaluation. A psycho-social evaluation, completed in person.
  3. Orientation. An interactive group session.
  4. Ongoing groups. Groups of 5 to 20 people, per State of Texas BIPP Accreditation Guidelines.

Completion requires the minimum 36-hour educational group sessions and appropriate participation. Program fees are explained at enrollment.
