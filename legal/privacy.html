Privacy

Client-supplied documentThis page is a placeholder. The privacy policy is The Capelli Institute's own legal document and must be supplied or approved by the Institute before launch (SCOPE.md Q9). The list below states what this site actually does, so the policy can be written against it.

What this website collects

  • The early access mailing list: an email address, only when you submit it on the launch notice page.
  • CEU workshop registration: name, email, phone, license details, chosen session, and any notes you add.
  • Workshop inquiries from organizations: organization, contact name, email, phone, and the details of your request.

What it does not do

  • No analytics, no advertising pixels, no social media beacons, no third-party scripts. Fonts are served from this site.
  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • The BIPP Education Series, Anger Resolution Series and Cognitive Programs pages collect nothing. Enrollment for those programs is in person.
  • Nothing resembling clinical information is requested anywhere on this site.

Purchases

Books and lectures are purchased through outside booksellers. This site does not take payment and does not hold payment details.
