4. Why this is not the same job as the clinic site

This matters more than any other section here, so it is stated up front rather than buried.

The clinic site is one page that is finished the day it launches. It has no forms, takes no money, and its content already existed. That is a genuinely different category of work.

The Institute site takes money and grows. That changes it in four specific ways:

  1. Editions are not one product. A Five Elemental Philosophy in paperback and in the signed leather Collector's Edition is two things for sale on one page, each with its own price, its own ISBN, and its own availability. Across five books that is roughly fifteen sellable items, and you are buying a hundred ISBNs, so the real number is larger.
  2. Two catalogs that keep growing. Books and Lectures in Print are both lists you add to. That means the site has to let you add a title without calling me. A site where every new book is a phone call to a developer is the wrong build for an author.
  3. Taking payment brings its own requirements. A checkout needs a payment account, receipts, refund terms, a privacy policy, and sales tax handling on physical books. None of that exists on the clinic site because it sells nothing.
  4. CEU registration plus the calendar. You update your Outlook calendar, the dates appear on the site. That is two connections, not a page.

On features alone this is several times the clinic project. On what happens after launch it is a different category, because the clinic site is done and this one is designed to keep going.
